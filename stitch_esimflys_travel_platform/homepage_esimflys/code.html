Common Questions.

Is my phone compatible? expand_more
Most modern smartphones released after 2019 (iPhone 11+, Google Pixel 3+, Samsung S20+) support eSIM technology. You can check by dialling *#06# - if you see an EID number, you're good to go.
Can I keep my WhatsApp number? expand_more
Yes! Your eSIM is for data only. Your existing SIM card can remain in your phone for calls and SMS, and your WhatsApp, iMessage, and Telegram will continue working with your original number.
When should I install the eSIM? expand_more
We recommend installing it before you depart while you have a stable Wi-Fi connection. You can keep the line turned off until you land at your destination.
