How to check manually

Quick steps to verify eSIM compatibility in your settings.

phone_iphone

iOS (iPhone)

  • 01

    Go to Settings

    Open your device's settings app.

  • 02

    Tap Cellular or Mobile Data

    Look for the connectivity section.

  • 03

    Check for "Add eSIM"

    If you see "Add eSIM" or "Add Cellular Plan", your phone is compatible.

android

Android

  • 01

    Go to Settings

    Access your device's configuration menu.

  • 02

    Network & Internet

    Navigate to Mobile Network or Connections.

  • 03

    Check for "Download a SIM"

    Look for "SIMs +" or "Download a SIM instead?".
