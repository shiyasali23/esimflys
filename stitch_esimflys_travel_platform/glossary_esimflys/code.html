eSIM terms, demystified

Master the language of global connectivity. Everything you need to know about eSIM technology in one place.

search
  • All

A

APN

Core

Access Point Name. The gateway between a GSM, GPRS, 3G or 4G mobile network and another computer network, frequently the public Internet.

See also: MNO

E

eSIM

Essential

Embedded SIM. A digital SIM that allows you to activate a cellular plan from your carrier without having to use a physical nano-SIM.

See also: QR code

EID

Embedded Identity Document. A unique serial number attached to the eSIM hardware soldered into your device.

I

ICCID

Integrated Circuit Card Identifier. A unique 18 to 22-digit number used to identify the current SIM card profile globally.

IMEI

International Mobile Equipment Identity. A unique number to identify mobile phones, as well as some satellite phones.

L & M

LTE

Long-Term Evolution. A standard for high-speed wireless communication for mobile devices and data terminals.

MNO

Mobile Network Operator. A provider of wireless communications services that owns or controls all the elements necessary to sell and deliver services.

MVNO

Mobile Virtual Network Operator. A wireless communications services provider that does not own the wireless network infrastructure.

R & V

Roaming

The ability for a cellular customer to automatically make and receive voice calls, send and receive data, or access other services when travelling outside the geographical coverage area of the home network.

VPN

Virtual Private Network. Encrypts your internet traffic and hides your online identity, making it more difficult for third parties to track your activities online.

VoLTE

New

Voice over Long-Term Evolution. A high-speed wireless communication standard for mobile phones and data terminals.
