A real customer installed correctly, had no internet for an hour, and concluded the product was broken: the profile was on the phone (smdpStatus INSTALLATION, iPhone 13) and had never attached to a network, because these plans roam on a foreign IMSI and Data Roaming was off. This block used to be written with {# #}, which in Django is a SINGLE-LINE comment. As a multi-line block it is not a comment at all — it rendered verbatim into the email, and customers were reading this engineering note as body copy. That is the bug this rewrite starts from; see esim-ready template tests. {% endcomment %}
